# Photograph

`hero-1600.jpg` and `hero-960.jpg` are crops of **"Perseverance's Selfie with Ingenuity"** (PIA24542): NASA's Perseverance rover on Mars with the Ingenuity helicopter, taken on 6 April 2021, the mission's sol 46, by the WATSON camera on the rover's arm.

- **Credit:** NASA/JPL-Caltech/MSSS.
- **Source:** <https://photojournal.jpl.nasa.gov/catalog/PIA24542>
